SECTION 02 / EDUCATIONAL BREAKDOWN
Difference Between Wireframe, UI Design, and Portfolio
1
Wireframe

The skeleton and low-fidelity structural blueprint. Outlines content hierarchy, navigation, and user flow before any styling.

Purpose: Structure & Flow
Stage: Early Discovery
Focus: Layout & Function
Output: Grayscale Schematic
2
UI Design

The skin that transforms wireframes into polished interfaces. Adds brand identity, colors, typography, imagery, and effects.

Purpose: Visual Style & Brand
Stage: Mid / Late Phase
Focus: Look & Feel
Output: High-Fidelity Assets
3
Portfolio

The gallery that showcases your body of work, problem-solving journey, and design results to clients and employers.

Purpose: Showcase Past Work
Stage: Ongoing / Continuous
Focus: Skills & Case Studies
Output: Curated Showcase Site
