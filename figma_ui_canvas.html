THE DESIGN SPECTRUM

Difference Between Wireframe, UI Design, and Portfolio

Source: MockFlow & Fruto Design Research
📐

1. Wireframe

The Skeleton: Low-fidelity structural blueprint. Outlines content hierarchy, navigation, and user flow before any visual styling.

Purpose: Plan structure & flow
Stage: Early design discovery
Focus: Functionality & layout
Output: Low-fidelity blueprint
🎨

2. UI Design

The Skin: Visual styling stage. Transforms the wireframe with brand-aligned colors, typography, imagery, and paint aesthetics.

Purpose: Add visual style & branding
Stage: Mid / late design
Focus: Look, feel & emotion
Output: High-fidelity visuals
🏛

3. Portfolio

The Gallery: Curated collection of your design work, case studies, and proven results showcasing your skills to employers.

Purpose: Showcase past work
Stage: Ongoing evolution
Focus: Skills & results
Output: Curated project website
